01 Biography

I picked up guitar as a teenager in Buenos Aires, covering grunge, punk, and hard rock in my parents' garage. In the early 2000s I formed the indie rock band Olaguer, shaped by Argentina's Sónico movement — Soda Stereo, Babasónicos, Los Brujos.

After school and a long trip through South America, I came home moved by the continent's music and field-recorded sounds, and studied classical guitar and charango.

In 2011 I moved to California and began experimenting with electronic. Those influences converged into my solo project — right here.
